## Effects of cosmetics on health: headaches

Headaches are listed in the source as an effect of cosmetics on health. *(p. 58)*

## Effects of cosmetics on health: hair problems

Extensive use of chemical based hair products could lead to dandruff, scalp redness, thinning of hair, and even loss of hair. Long term use of hair color could also lead to hair discoloration. *(p. 58)*

## Effects of cosmetics on health: acne

Some types of makeup which are in the form of liquids and creams clog the pores in the skin. This leads to the formation of blackheads, which when not cleaned regularly can form acne. *(p. 58)*

## Effects of cosmetics on health: skin allergies and parabens

Chemicals known as **parabens**, which include ethyl-paraben, butyl-paraben, and isopropyl-paraben, are used as preservatives to prevent bacterial growth in cosmetics. Parabens can cause various allergic reactions like skin irritation, blotches, and blemishes on the skin. *(p. 58)*

## Effects of cosmetics on health: eye infections

Layers of eye makeup can be damaging to the eyes as they can also slip into the eyes through the corners, causing irritation. *(p. 58)*

## Effects of cosmetics on health: premature aging

When skin products are used for a longer period of time, the chemicals present tend to permanently damage the skin. With time, skin ageing signs like wrinkles or patchiness on the face and body could begin to show. While makeup does help hide or cover flaws in the skin, the long term effects could be counterproductive. Also, considering how big the anti-ageing products market is, cosmetic companies have no incentive to reduce the ageing effects of makeup. *(p. 58)*

## Effects of cosmetics on health: cancer and toxic ingredients to avoid

Many of the chemical based cosmetics available in stores today contain toxic ingredients that could cause cancer. With regulations in place, there is testing done on the ingredients before the products can be sold. The source advises trying to avoid products with the following ingredients: *(p. 58)*

- Formaldehyde and formaldehyde-releasing preservatives
- Phenacetin
- Coal tar
- Benzene
- Untreated or mildly treated mineral oils
- Ethylene oxide
- Chromium
- Cadmium and its compounds
- Arsenic
- Crystalline silica (or quartz)

## Effects of cosmetics on health: skin discoloration

Skin products like sunscreens, moisturizers, toners, and creams contain agents that bleach or darken the skin. Cosmetic products that use poor quality ingredients which have not been regulated can lead to skin discoloration. The effect could be patches, pigmentation, uneven skin tone, redness, and freckles. *(p. 58)*
